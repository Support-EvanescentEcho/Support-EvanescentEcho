### Hi, I'm Evanescent Echo 👋

Crafting intimate, secure tools to preserve life's fleeting moments before they fade away.

**How they're built**
- 🔒 **Local-first**: your data lives on your devices, not on a server. There is no server.
- 🗝️ **Really encrypted**: libsodium, with encrypted backups and media.
- 🔁 **Device-to-device sync** over your local network, no cloud in between.
- ♿ **Accessible**: built against WCAG 2.2 AA.
- 🚫 **No accounts, no ads, no tracking.**

**Apps**
- **[Hours Lived](https://support-evanescentecho.github.io/)**: a private diary for the hours worth remembering. Android & desktop. _Closed testing on Google Play._
